## Overview

The Lectric XP 3.0 is a compact folding e-bike with 20×3.0" fat tires rated for 20-30 PSI. Its short wheelbase and rear hub motor create a distinct weight bias that requires careful pressure adjustment for different loads.

**Stock tire specs:**
- **Size:** 20×3.0"
- **PSI range:** 20-30 PSI (sidewall max)
- **Casing:** Tubed (standard)
- **Bike weight:** 64 lbs

## Solo Riding

For solo riders on pavement, start with 23F/27R for average weight (180 lbs). The XP 3.0's rear hub motor and battery place significant weight on the rear wheel, requiring 3-4 PSI higher than front.

**Light riders (140-160 lbs):** Drop to 21F/24R for comfort without sacrificing efficiency.

**Heavy riders (220-250 lbs):** Increase to 25F/29R. You're near sidewall max, so check pressure before every ride.

## Passenger Mode

Adding a passenger (rear rack seat) puts 100% of their weight on the rear tire. For a 180 lb rider + 120 lb passenger:

- **Front:** 24 PSI (slight increase for stability)
- **Rear:** 29 PSI (near max due to combined load)

**Critical:** Never exceed 30 PSI. If you're maxed out, reduce speed on rough terrain to prevent pinch flats.

## Cargo Loading

The rear rack (75 lb capacity) shifts weight distribution significantly:

- **20 lbs cargo:** +1 PSI rear
- **40 lbs cargo:** +2 PSI rear
- **60+ lbs cargo:** +3 PSI rear, consider front +1 PSI for balance

Example: 180 lb rider + 50 lbs groceries = 24F/28R on pavement.

## Terrain Adjustments

### Pavement (100%)
Use baseline PSI from table above. Optimize for efficiency and comfort.

### Mixed (pavement + gravel)
Drop 2-3 PSI overall. Example: 21F/24R for 180 lb rider.

### Dirt Trails
Drop 3-4 PSI for traction. Example: 20F/23R. Watch for rim strikes on rocks.

### Sand & Snow
Drop to tire minimum (20 PSI front, 22 PSI rear) for flotation. Reduce speed to avoid overheating motor.

## Tubeless Conversion

If you convert to tubeless:
- Drop 1-2 PSI overall
- Run 19F/22R for 180 lb rider on pavement
- Use sealant rated for e-bike speeds (25+ mph)

## Pressure Check Frequency

- **Daily commuters:** Check weekly
- **Weekend riders:** Check before each ride
- **Cargo/passenger use:** Check before every loaded trip
- **Temperature changes:** Recheck when temps drop >20°F

## Common Mistakes

1. **Matching front/rear PSI:** Rear needs 3-4 PSI more due to weight bias.
2. **Ignoring cargo weight:** 40 lbs cargo = +2 PSI rear minimum.
3. **Overinflating for range:** Gains are minimal; comfort and traction suffer.
4. **Using gas station gauges:** Often inaccurate below 30 PSI. Use a digital gauge.

## Safety Warnings

- ⚠️ Never exceed 30 PSI (sidewall max)
- ⚠️ Below 20 PSI increases pinch flat risk
- ⚠️ Check valve stem tightness on folding bikes (vibration can loosen caps)
- ⚠️ Inspect tires for cracks/wear every 500 miles
