## The Hidden Range Killer

One day your commute takes 15% of your e-bike's battery, and the next week it takes 20%. You haven't gained weight, the weather hasn't changed, and your route is identical. What happened?

More often than not, the culprit is hidden in your tires. 

E-bikes are heavy, often weighing between 55 and 80 lbs. This immense continuous weight forces the tires to slowly leak air over time—a process called permeation. If you aren't checking your tire pressure regularly, you are slowly bleeding battery range and performance.

## 1. The Rolling Resistance Tax

When an e-bike tire is under-inflated by just 10 PSI, its "contact patch" (the amount of rubber touching the road) increases dramatically. While more rubber on the road can improve grip in certain off-road scenarios, on pavement, it acts like glue.

This is known as **rolling resistance**. To overcome this sticky drag, your e-bike's hub or mid-drive motor has to work significantly harder.
* **Battery Drain:** Studies show that a 20% drop in PSI can reduce your overall battery range by up to 15%. If your bike boasts a 50-mile range, under-inflated tires just stole 7.5 miles from you.
* **Top Speed Loss:** On Class 3 e-bikes, low pressure can prevent you from reaching your top pedal-assist speed without exhausting yourself.

## 2. The "Sluggish" Handling Feeling

Have you ever turned the handlebars of your e-bike and felt a momentary delay before the bike actually leaned into the turn? Or perhaps the bike feels "wobbly" at higher speeds?

This is called **tire squirm**. When PSI is too low, the sidewalls of the tire lack the structural rigidity to support the lateral forces of cornering. The tire physically flexes and rolls underneath the rim before the bike changes direction. On a heavy e-bike traveling at 20mph, tire squirm isn't just annoying; it's a safety hazard.

## 3. Premature Tire Wear

E-bike tires aren't cheap. High-quality, puncture-resistant rubber like the Schwalbe Marathon E-Plus can cost upwards of $60 per tire. 

Running low tire pressure causes the center of the tire to bow inward slightly, shifting the contact patch to the softer shoulders of the tread. Because e-bikes generate massive torque from a standstill, accelerating on severely under-inflated tires will rapidly scrub the rubber off the casing, cutting the lifespan of your expensive tires in half.

## The 60-Second Maintenance Routine

To prevent your fast, fun e-bike from turning into a sluggish, battery-draining chore, implement this simple maintenance routine:

1. **The Weekly Gauge:** Buy a high-quality floor pump with an integrated pressure gauge. Check the pressure *every single week*. E-bike tires can naturally lose 1-3 PSI per week.
2. **The Thumb Test (Pre-Ride):** Before you throw your leg over the bike, press your thumbs hard into the top of the rear tire. It should feel almost as hard as an apple. If it feels like a ripe orange, do not ride until you inflate it.
3. **Use the Calculator:** Don't just guess your pressure. Whether you're riding a [RadWagon 4](/models/rad-power-radwagon-4) or a [Tern GSD](/models/tern-gsd-s10), proper tire pressure is critical.
