## Part 1: Detailed Content Calendar (Step-by-Step)

### Week 1: The "Click-Through" Rescue Mission
**Focus:** Fix the 0% CTR on your #1 rankings. You are ranking but not getting the click.
**Target Pages:** Propella, Tern, Velotric.

#### Task 1.1: Propella Page Overhaul
* **Data Insight:** Ranking #1.67 for "propella tire pressure" (12 impressions, 0 clicks).
* **Step 1:** **Change Page Title** to: `Propella E-Bike Tire Pressure Calculator & Charts (7S, 9S, Mini)`
    * *Why:* "Calculator" and specific models imply immediate utility.
* **Step 2:** **Rewrite Meta Description** to:
    * `"Stop guessing your PSI. Enter your rider weight in our free calculator to get the exact tire pressure for Propella 7S, 9S, and Mini e-bikes."`
* **Step 3:** **Above-the-Fold Edit:** Ensure the **Calculator** or a **Quick Reference Table** is the very first thing visible. Do not bury the answer under 500 words of intro text.

#### Task 1.2: Tern GSD Optimization
* **Data Insight:** Ranking for both "tire" and "tyre" spellings (#6.4 position).
* **Step 1:** **Content Update:** Scan your text and ensure you use the word "Tyre" (British spelling) at least once in a subheader, e.g., `H2: Recommended Tern GSD Tyre Pressure`.
* **Step 2:** **Schema Check:** Ensure your table is wrapped in HTML `<table>` tags so Google can pull it into a Featured Snippet.

## Part 2: Detailed Data Breakdown & Action Steps

### Cluster A: The "Lectric" Group (High Volume, Mixed Intent)
* **Queries:** `lectric xpress 750 tire size`, `lectric xp 3.0 tire pressure`, `lectric xpedition weight`, `lectric tire psi`.
* **Analysis:** Users are looking for specs (Size) just as much as Pressure.
* **Actionable Step:** Audit all Lectric tables. Ensure they have three distinct columns: **Model Name**, **Tire Size (e.g., 20x3.0)**, and **Recommended PSI**.

### Cluster B: The "Aventon" Group (New Model Interest)
* **Queries:** `aventon level 3 tire pressure`, `aventon pace 500.3`, `aventon abound tire size`.
* **Analysis:** "Level 3" is the breakout star. It's a new bike, so competition is low.
* **Actionable Step:** Write a dedicated paragraph about the **Aventon Abound** (cargo bike). Cargo bike owners are obsessed with tire pressure because of the heavy loads. This is a high-engagement niche.

### Cluster C: The "Rad Power" Group (Legacy Models)
* **Queries:** `radwagon 4 tire pressure`, `radwagon 4 tire size`, `radrunner plus`.
* **Analysis:** Traffic is for older/established models (`RadWagon 4`).
* **Actionable Step:** Mention the specific **custom tire size** of the RadWagon 4 (22" x 3.0"). It's a weird size, and owners often panic about finding replacements. Write a tip: *"Note: The RadWagon 4 uses a unique 22-inch tire. Stick to the official PSI of..."*

### Cluster D: The "UK/Global" Group
* **Queries:** `tern gsd tyre pressure`, `gocycle tyre pressure`.
* **Analysis:** "Tyre" with a "y" indicates UK/Europe traffic.
* **Actionable Step:** On pages for **Tern** and **GoCycle**, explicitly mention "Bar" units alongside PSI (e.g., "50 PSI / 3.4 Bar"). European users prefer Bar.

### Cluster E: The "Fat Tire" Generic Group
* **Queries:** `fat tire bike tire pressure`, `fat bike tire pressure`.
* **Analysis:** You are ranking #54-61. This is too broad to win yet.
* **Actionable Step:** Ignore this for now. Focus on winning the specific brands (Lectric, Aventon) first. The authority you build there will eventually lift these generic rankings.
